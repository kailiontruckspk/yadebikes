What batteries do YAD vehicles use?

Our price list shows 60V and 72V battery packs, from 60V 25Ah up to 72V 38Ah. Our three-wheelers use 60V 25Ah graphene batteries.

Scooters carry a one year warranty. The V2 and H1 three-wheelers carry 12 months on the motor. Terms and conditions apply.
